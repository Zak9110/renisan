# PRODUCT

## One line

An Iraqi-only platform, mainly for students, that cuts through the noise of scattered scholarship posts: verified, deduplicated, Iraqi-eligibility-tagged, matched to each student, with verified peers who help others apply for a small fee. Over time it becomes a movement: students, graduates, and ambassadors abroad.

## Problem

- Opportunities are posted everywhere (Facebook, Telegram, TikTok, Instagram). It is crowded, repetitive, often stale, sometimes scams.
- Most listings do not say whether Iraqis are eligible, or what an Iraqi student needs (recognized degree, English proof, documents).
- Good help exists (students who won scholarships) but is hard to find and hard to trust.

## Users

1. **Grade 12 / pre-university** (many are minors): admissions eligibility, first scholarships. Seasonal hook.
2. **Undergraduates**: scholarships, exchanges, internships, tutoring.
3. **Graduates and students abroad**: master's/PhD funding; become helpers and ambassadors.
4. **Editors/curators** (internal): keep the feed correct.
5. **Sponsors** (universities, language centers, programs): pay for labeled visibility.

## Pillars and phases

| Phase | Pillar                                                              | Notes                                       |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Curated opportunities feed + explainable matching + Telegram alerts | Core, free forever                          |
| 1b    | Admissions eligibility checker (federal first, KRG second)          | Needs cut-off data work                     |
| 2     | Verified helpers + paid coaching services + bookings                | Small fixed packages, held payment, reviews |
| 3     | Ambassadors abroad, events/seminars, clubs                          | Only after real usage                       |

## Principles

- **Filtering, not posting.** The value is signal: fresh, verified, matched.
- **Information is free; personal help is paid.**
- **Explain every match.** GO / MAYBE / NO_GO with reasons and what to add to confirm.
- **Be honest about sponsors.** Labeled, never affecting eligibility or order.
- **Safety first for minors.**
- **Deliver where users are:** Telegram bot and share cards first; do not force daily web visits.
- **Design for slow, cheap mobile data and for RTL/LTR in four languages.**

## Non-goals (for now)

Open social network, real-time chat/DMs, native apps, AI chatbot, scraping other sites, study-abroad agency commissions.

## Success metrics (avoid vanity)

- Weekly active matched users and alert click-through rate
- Median age of published listings under 30 days; zero expired listings visible
- Share of listings with `iraqiEligibility` not "unclear" (target above 80%)
- Scam reports per 1,000 listing views (falling)
- Applied / "got it" outcomes reported
- Helper order completion rate, repeat orders, average rating
- Cost per active user via organic channels

## Brand and voice

Working name Renisan (guidance). Warm, direct, short sentences, trilingual from day one. Final name and domain are the founder's call.

## Business model (in order)

1. Grants fund the build and the free tier (Orange Corners / Five One Labs, US Embassy and IYLEP alumni grants, UNDP/GIZ). Realistic Year 1 total inflow including grants: roughly $8k–$30k; net commercial revenue $3k–$12k (modeled estimate, not a guarantee).
2. Labeled sponsored listings from universities and language centers (roughly $200–$700 per season each).
3. Helper marketplace take rate of 10–15% on paid coaching packages.
4. Later: premium filters/alerts at very low prices, white-label for NGOs.
   Never sell student data. Never let money change eligibility results.
