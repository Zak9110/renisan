# SAFEGUARDING AND TRUST

## Why this matters

Many users are 17–18, some from displaced or minority communities. Scholarship scams are common, and paid help invites misuse. Trust is the product.

## Scam and quality rules for listings

Red flags (reject or flag for review):

- Application, processing, redemption or "disbursement" fees for a scholarship
- Guaranteed awards or "money back" claims
- "Exclusive" scholarships offered by a single service
- Unsolicited "you have been selected" messages
- Pressure to pay or act today, or requests for bank/card details
- Official-sounding names with no verifiable provider or domain
  Real costs (tests, visas, courses) are shown as `costToApplicant`, never hidden.
  Verification checklist for editors: official domain, search "<name> scam/complaint", deadline confirmed on the official page, eligibility for Iraqi nationals confirmed or marked unclear.

## Helper rules (coaching, not ghostwriting)

- Helpers coach; they never write, rewrite or "polish" an applicant's essay or personal statement for submission, and never complete forms on their behalf.
- No guarantees, no "inside connections", no commission or finder's-fee influence on advice. Disclose any financial relationship with institutions.
- Services have a written scope and price; changes need buyer consent.
- Misrepresenting outcomes (claiming a scholarship they did not win) = permanent ban.

## Minors (under 18)

- No public profile, no discoverability, no open messages.
- Paid or 1:1 services need a recorded guardian consent (consent text in the user's language, timestamp, contact of guardian).
- Prefer group sessions. Platform-hosted sessions are recorded with disclosure; retention default 90 days unless disputed.
- Helpers and ambassadors must not ask minors to move to private channels.
- Age-appropriate language. A visible "report a problem" button on every page involving services.

## Reporting and escalation

- One-tap report on listings, helpers, ambassadors, reviews, messages in groups.
- Safeguarding concerns (grooming, harassment, coercion) go to a named safeguarding focal person within 24 hours, immediate removal of the account from services while investigating.
- Keep an incident log (audit log), not shared publicly.
- Plan a partnership with a child-protection-capable organization (founder to arrange) before Phase 2 launches.

## Privacy by design

- Collect minimum data. No ethnicity, religion, exact DOB, national ID numbers.
- IDP/displacement flag optional and private, used for matching only.
- Hash OTPs, rate limit, no sensitive data in logs. Export and delete account.
- Verification documents in private storage and auto-deleted 30 days after review.
- No third-party ad trackers. Explain data use in plain language in all four locales.

## Sponsorship honesty

- Sponsored items labeled and visually distinct. No effect on eligibility, matching, or editorial order.
- Never sell personal data. Share only anonymous aggregate statistics.

## Moderation of community content (Phase 3)

- Code of conduct in four languages. Pseudonymity option for non-adult-facing posts. Gender-aware moderation and harassment handling. Quick removal path, appeal path.

## Honesty about limits

Cut-offs are indicative history, scholarships change often, and the platform cannot guarantee outcomes. Say so clearly in the UI.
